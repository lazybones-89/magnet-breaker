Magnet Breaker - original music preview 01, adopted for v0.5.4
Composition, arrangement and synthesized instruments were created for this project.
No external music recording, sample library or purchased music asset is used.

Original sources: AI-bgm-preview-20261104/tools/compose.py and the four approved loop WAV files.
Web versions are Vorbis encodings of the full approved loops, with no arrangement,
tempo, pitch, duration or gain change. The original files remain preserved separately.

FFmpeg via imageio-ffmpeg 0.6.0 was used only as a local encoding/verification tool.
The encoder executable is not distributed in the game.
